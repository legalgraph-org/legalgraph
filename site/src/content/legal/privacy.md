## When you visit the site

The site is a set of static pages served by Cloudflare Pages. Every time your browser asks for a page, the server necessarily receives technical information about the request:

- your IP address,
- the date and time,
- the page or file requested, and whether it was delivered,
- the referring page, if your browser sends it,
- your browser and operating system (the user agent).

This is used only to deliver the pages and to keep the site secure and available, for example against attacks and abuse. We do not combine it with other data, build profiles from it or use it to identify you.

**Legal basis:** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a site that works and is secure.

**Recipient:** Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) hosts the site and delivers it through its network on our behalf, as a processor under a data processing agreement (Article 28 GDPR). Cloudflare may process the data in the United States. It is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Article 45 GDPR); the agreement also contains the EU standard contractual clauses (Article 46 GDPR).

**Storage:** Cloudflare keeps request logs only as long as it needs them for delivery and security, as described in its own privacy policy. We do not keep request logs of our own.

## Settings stored in your browser

A few pages remember choices you make, so you don't have to make them again: whether law texts show side by side or stacked, whether the details panel of the graph is folded, which text the graph shows (original, translation or both), and how many entries a list shows per page. They are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they contain nothing that identifies you.

Storing them is strictly necessary to provide the setting you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), so it needs no consent. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

**There are no cookies**, no tracking pixels, no analytics and no third-party scripts on legalgraph.org. The fonts and all other files come from the site itself.

## When you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and what you write to answer you and to handle your request, for example a correction to the graph.

**Legal basis:** Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering), or Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message is about a contract.

**Storage:** we delete the messages when the matter is settled, unless we must keep them by law (for example commercial or tax records).

Our email is hosted by a provider acting as our processor under a data processing agreement.

Law texts and sources link to official portals and other sites, for example EUR-Lex, gesetze-im-internet.de or legislation.gov.uk. When you follow a link, the other site's own privacy policy applies.

## Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, and to correct, erase or restrict it, or hand it over to you (Articles 15–18 and 20 GDPR), and you can object to our use of it (Article 21). In practice we hold very little: we can't tell who you are from a page visit (Article 11 GDPR), so these rights mostly concern emails you have sent us. Write to [data-protection-inquiries@aimix.io](mailto:data-protection-inquiries@aimix.io).

You can also complain to a data protection authority (Article 77 GDPR), for example the Berlin authority responsible for us: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, [www.datenschutz-berlin.de](https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de).

## No obligation, no automated decisions

You don't have to provide any personal data to use legalgraph.org. We make no decisions about you by automated means, including profiling.
