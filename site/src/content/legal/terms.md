## 1. What legalgraph is, and what it is not

legalgraph is an open knowledge graph of the laws that tell websites what to show and do. It links every statement to the words of the law it comes from: the official text, its provisions, translations, and rules read from them.

It is **structured research, not legal advice**. In particular:

- The **rules** (what a provision means for a business, and when it applies) are our interpretation. They are drafts until a lawyer reviews them, and they can be incomplete or wrong.
- **Translations** made by machine are labelled as such and stay drafts until a person reviews them. Only the original text, as the official portal publishes it, is the law.
- **Coverage** is selective. That the graph records no duty for a situation does not mean that no law imposes one.
- The **checklist** shows which rules in the graph match your answers. It cannot replace advice on your particular case.

Before relying on any entry, read the official text it links to, and ask a qualified lawyer where it matters.
