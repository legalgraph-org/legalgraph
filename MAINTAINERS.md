# Maintainers

Maintainers review pull requests, merge them, and decide on changes to the ontology. They are
listed in the [legalgraph-org](https://github.com/legalgraph-org) organisation on GitHub.

legalgraph was started by [Aimix GmbH](https://aimix.io), whose team maintains it today.

## Becoming a maintainer

The graph is only as good as the people who check it, so we want the team to grow beyond its
founders. Contributors who have made substantial, careful contributions over time (laws, countries,
court decisions, reviews of other people's pull requests, translations) can be invited to become
maintainers. Knowing the law of a country well, or the official sources it is published in,
counts as much as code.

There is no application form: keep contributing, and the maintainers will reach out. If you would
like to take on more, say so in an issue.

## What maintainers look for in a review

- Every new statement traces to an official text, or is marked as a reading (Draft).
- Quotes match the official wording; annotations sit on the right paragraph.
- New terms fit the ontology, and changes to the model were agreed in an issue first.
- `make check` passes.
